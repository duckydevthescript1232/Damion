Before you order

Questions artists usually ask.

You do not need to know technical terms, your BPM or your key before contacting me.

Read every answer
Which service do I need?

Tell me what you already have and what still sounds unfinished. I can point you toward the closest option before you order.

Can I send an unfinished song?

Yes. A voice memo, demo, vocal recording or reference track can be enough to start a conversation.

How do I send project files?

Your project information and file link stay connected to the order. Large files can be shared with a trusted Drive or Dropbox link.

What happens after ordering?

Your order receives a private room where the project status, messages and final delivery can stay together.

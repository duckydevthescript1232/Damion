## Content rules

- Record vertically in 9:16.
- Let viewers hear a result during the first two seconds.
- Keep the most important text away from the top and bottom interface areas.
- Show the real FL Studio session or real artwork when possible.
- Use captions because many viewers start with low volume.
- Never invent customers, reviews, results or urgency.
- Ask permission before sharing client audio or project screens.
- One video should communicate one idea.
- Finish with one call to action, not five.

## Repeatable video structure

1. **Hook, 0–2 seconds:** Play the strongest before/after moment or ask a direct question.
2. **Proof, 2–8 seconds:** Show the FL Studio project and explain one change.
3. **Result, 8–13 seconds:** Play the finished section again.
4. **Action, final 2 seconds:** “Hear more at damionmusic.nl.”

## Personal outreach

Only contact artists whose work you genuinely listened to. Never send the same message to hundreds of people.

### First message

Hey [name], I listened to [track/title]. I especially liked [specific detail]. I’m a producer working with independent artists, and I noticed one area where the track might become even stronger: [short useful observation]. No pressure to buy anything—I thought the feedback might help.

### If they respond positively

Glad it helps. If you ever want another set of ears, I offer focused beat feedback, mixing, mastering and production through damionmusic.nl. If you send me what stage the song is at, I can tell you which option fits before you order.

### Collaboration message

Hey [name], your style on [specific release] stood out to me. I produce and finish music for independent artists. If you’re open to it, I’d like to make a short private production idea inspired by that direction. No public posting without your permission.

## Weekly community routine

- Leave five genuine comments on artists’ work each day.
- Answer every relevant comment on your own posts.
- Turn good questions into the next video.
- Save frequently asked questions for the website FAQ.
- Ask completed customers for an honest review through the official order flow.
- Never purchase followers, comments or fake reviews.

## Paid promotion rule

Do not advertise a generic logo or static flyer. First identify an organic video that causes real profile visits, questions or website clicks. Then test that exact video with a small budget.

Suggested first test:

- Budget: €5 per day for five days.
- Creative: strongest before/after video.
- Destination: the matching service or homepage with a UTM link.
- Keep only if it produces useful visits, support conversations or carts.

## What to measure

Record these numbers once per week:

- Videos published
- Three-second and complete video views
- Profile visits
- Website clicks by UTM source
- Audio preview plays
- Service views
- Support chats opened
- Support messages sent
- Add-to-cart actions
- Checkout starts
- Completed orders

Do not judge promotion only by views. Ten relevant artists who listen and ask questions are more valuable than thousands of unrelated views.

Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you contact the site, join email updates or place an order.

Information collected

When you contact Damiøn, join email updates or place an order, the site may collect your name, email address, country, artist name, project name, project notes, order contents and payment status. PayPal processes payment details; Damiøn does not receive or store your card number.

Why it is used

Information is used to respond to messages, process and manage orders, deliver purchased services, prevent fraud, keep basic business records and, when you explicitly opt in, send occasional studio updates, service news, tips or special offers.

Email marketing

If you join the mailing list, your email address is sent to Mailchimp to manage the subscription and marketing emails. The signup uses confirmation by email. You can unsubscribe using the link in any marketing email.

Service providers

The site uses Supabase for backend data storage, PayPal for payments and Mailchimp for optional email marketing. Those providers process data under their own privacy terms.

Retention

Order and contact information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for project delivery, customer support, accounting, dispute handling and legal obligations. Mailing-list data is kept while you remain subscribed or as needed to respect an unsubscribe request.

Your choices

You can ask about access, correction or deletion of personal data where applicable. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time by unsubscribing. Some order information may need to be retained when required by law.

Security

Payment creation and price verification happen on the server. Sensitive payment and Mailchimp credentials are not placed in the public website code.

Contact

Privacy requests can be sent through the Contact page.

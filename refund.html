Refund Policy

How cancellations, revisions and refund requests are handled.

Before work starts

If you placed an order by mistake, contact Damiøn as soon as possible. Where a refund is legally available and work has not started, the order can be reviewed for cancellation.

After work starts

Music production services are customised digital services. Once work has started or custom work has been delivered, refund eligibility may be limited, subject to applicable consumer law and any rights that cannot be waived.

Revisions

If the result needs changes within the purchased scope, use the revisions included with your package before requesting a refund. Extra revisions can be purchased when needed.

Technical problems

If delivered files are corrupted or incomplete, contact Damiøn so the files can be replaced or corrected.

Payment disputes

Please contact Damiøn first so the issue can be investigated. PayPal’s dispute process remains available according to PayPal’s rules.
